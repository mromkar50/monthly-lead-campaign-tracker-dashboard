Verified Mathematical Logic

Campaign Date & Monthly Lead Gap Formula Guide

1. Days in Month Detection: Automatically detects 31 days (Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Aug, Oct, Dec), 30 days (Apr, Jun, Sep, Nov), or 28/29 days (Feb).
2. Elapsed & Remaining Days: If Start = 12 Oct & Today = 21 Oct → Elapsed = 21 - 12 = 9 days | Remaining = 31 - 21 = 10 days left | Active Window = 19 days.
3. Pro-Rata Expected Leads Till Today: round(Target Leads × (Elapsed Days ÷ Active Campaign Days)).
4. Leads Gap in Month & % Need to Achieve: Leads Gap = max(0, Target - Achieved) | Req/Day = Leads Gap ÷ Days Left | % Need to Achieve = (Leads Gap ÷ Target) × 100.
